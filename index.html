Frequently Asked Questions

Is there transport between the hotels and wedding venues?

Yes, dedicated guest shuttle buses will run between ITC Kohenur and Fort Grand (Wedding Muhurtham venue) starting at 7:00 AM on December 14.

What is the dress code for non-South Indian / international guests?

Any modest formal attire or Indian ethnic wear is warmly welcomed! Bright yellows/florals for Haldi, glamorous party attire for Sangeeth, traditional silk kurta or saree for Pelli, and suit or formal dress for Reception.

Are dietary preferences catered for?

Absolutely! Pure vegetarian traditional Telugu meals will be served for Haldi and Pelli. The Sangeeth and Reception will feature multi-cuisine vegetarian, non-vegetarian, and Jain-friendly options.
